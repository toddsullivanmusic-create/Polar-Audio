# Polar Audio — Go-to-Market Playbook

## Brand

**Polar Audio** — "Sound you can see." Apple-grade dark-glass interfaces, one accent colour per product, a living backdrop that breathes with the audio. Every product opens on one screen; nothing is more than one click deep. The brand promise is *engine first*: every launch asset leads with audio, never with a feature list.

| Product | Accent | One-liner | Hero claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AURA** | Electric blue | The reverb that makes everything sound expensive. | 8-line modulated FDN with four colour circuits, true shimmer, and built-in ducking. |
| **GLOW** | Amber | Four analog circuits. One knob to taste. | Tube / Tape / Transformer / Crunch modelled as *different* nonlinearities, 4× oversampled, with Focus to aim the colour. |
| **McGEE** | Mint | The underwater synth. | Alias-free analog oscillators and a transistor ladder, then the texture stage the lo-fi records actually use: cassette wow & flutter, 12-bit crunch, muffle, tape saturation, haze chorus, a dirty gated room. One screen, one vibe. |
| **SANCTUARY** | Lavender | Pads in every key. Change key without a gap. | The first *instrument* for worship pads inside Logic: infinite sustain, seamless key crossfade, mod-wheel swell, one-tap fade. |

## Launch sequence (90 days)

**Weeks 1–2 — Seed.** 30 beta keys to worship keys players and mix engineers with YouTube/IG audiences (target: 10k–200k followers; micro-creators convert better than big names). Ask for *one* 60-second before/after, not a review.

**Week 3 — Launch SANCTUARY first.** It is the only product with no direct competitor, so it earns press on its own ("finally, worship pads as a real Logic instrument"). Launch price $49. Target channels: worship-tech YouTube (Churchfront, Worship Tutorials-style), the MultiTracks / Planning Center communities, Facebook groups for worship keys players, MainStage user groups.

**Week 5 — AURA + GLOW together** as "The Mix Pair", with a 14-day trial and a $79 pair price. These are the volume products; marketing is 100 % audio demos: vocal, drum bus, piano, synth pad, 15 s each, dry → wet, captioned.

**Week 8 — McGEE** aimed at the indie / bedroom-pop / alt-R&B producer who wants the Mk.gee-adjacent "underwater" palette without a cassette deck. Launch with 20-second "one knob" shorts (Wobble, Lo-fi, Room) — the texture controls demo themselves. Describe the sound, never claim artist endorsement: "lo-fi cassette texture", "underwater keys", not a name.

**Week 10 — Polar Collection bundle** at $149 intro, Black-Friday-adjacent.

## Pages

Every product page follows the same layout: autoplaying muted UI loop at the top (the backdrop breathing is the hook), a *Listen* strip of 4–6 A/B clips under it, three feature cards max, specs, price with trial button. No walls of text.

## Content that sells sound-first products

1. **A/B clips** — the single best converter. Every clip: same source, dry for 4 s, wet for 4 s, labelled with the preset name so the viewer can reproduce it.
2. **"One knob" shorts** — 20-second vertical videos of one parameter moving while the backdrop reacts. Share the GUI animation; it *is* the marketing.
3. **Presets with names people search for** — "Vocal Hall", "Tape Bus Glue", "Sunday Warm", "Spontaneous Flow". Preset names show up in walkthrough videos and become search terms.
4. **Artist-template cross-sell** — a free Logic template "Sunday Set" using SANCTUARY + AURA + GLOW on a typical worship session (pads, vocal, bus).

## Pricing tactics

- Intro pricing for 30 days, then list. Never discount below intro again except Black Friday (max 50 % of list).
- Loyalty: owners of any Polar product get the bundle completion price.
- Trials: 14 days, full features, email licence (no iLok). Trial start is the KPI to watch; expect 8–15 % trial→paid for sound-driven products.
- Education / church pricing: 30 % with a .org or church email — it builds goodwill in a community that talks to each other.

## Channel notes for the church market

- Worship producers buy on *trust* and *Sunday reliability*. Lead with "it will not drop out, it has no sample loading, it works offline, it is one screen."
- Show it on a MacBook on a keyboard stand, not in a studio.
- The killer demo is a *live key change*: play in D, worship leader calls G, tap G on the wheel, nothing stops.
- Partner angle: offer SANCTUARY as the "pads" layer to MultiTracks / Loop Community template makers; it replaces their 15-minute audio files.

## KPIs

Trial starts, trial→paid %, bundle attach rate, refund rate (< 2 %), preset-video views, church-pricing uptake.
